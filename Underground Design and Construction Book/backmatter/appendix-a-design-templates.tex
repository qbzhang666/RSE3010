\chapter{Design Calculation Templates}
\label{app:templates}

This appendix collects blank recording sheets for the derivations that recur
through the book. Their purpose is auditability: a completed sheet shows not
only the value adopted but the inputs, the judgement applied and the date, so
that a reviewer can reconstruct the reasoning and a later designer can see
what would change if the ground turned out differently.

The templates provided are a rock mass rating scoring sheet, a $Q$-system
scoring sheet, a Geological Strength Index selection record, a Hoek--Brown
parameter derivation sheet, a deformation modulus selection sheet recording
the expressions compared and the value adopted, and an equivalent
Mohr--Coulomb fitting sheet recording the confining stress range over which
the fit was made.

Each sheet carries fields for the data source, the assessor and the date, and
space for the reasoning behind any value that departs from a straightforward
reading of the chart. That last field is the one that matters. A rating
recorded without its justification cannot be reviewed, and cannot be revised
with confidence when the ground exposed at the face differs from the ground
assumed at design.

The derivations these sheets support are set out in
Chapter~\ref{ch:classification} for the classification systems and
Chapter~\ref{ch:rock-mass-parameters} for the parameter derivation.
