\chapter*{List of Symbols}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{List of Symbols}
\markboth{List of Symbols}{List of Symbols}
\label{ch:symbols}

The symbols below are those that appear in more than one chapter. A symbol
local to a single method is defined at first use and does not appear here.

Several letters carry more than one established meaning in the literature this
book draws on, and the distinction is kept rather than suppressed. For the
letter $k$: $k$ alone is the in-situ stress ratio, $k_\phi$ the Mohr--Coulomb
coefficient, $k_s$ a support stiffness, and $K$ the trough width parameter of
settlement analysis. For the letter $a$: $a$ is an opening radius and $a_{HB}$
the Hoek--Brown exponent. The letter $s$ is a Hoek--Brown constant and also a
steel-set spacing, $Q$ is the rock mass quality index and also an anchor
load--deformation constant, and $K$ is additionally the elastic bulk modulus at
its single use in Chapter~\ref{ch:rock-mechanics}. And for rock mass strength: $\scm$ is the
Hoek--Brown value while $\sigma_{cm,MC}$ is the unconfined strength of a
Mohr--Coulomb material --- in the worked example of
Chapter~\ref{ch:ccm} the latter is about a third larger at $\GSI = 60$ and
about four times larger at $\GSI = 30$. Where both readings of a letter occur
in the same chapter the subscripted form is used, and in every case the
expression states which quantity it takes.
